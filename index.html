02 / HOW I THINK

Curiosity first.
Evidence always.

I enjoy understanding how businesses work beneath the headline numbers. My approach is to ask better questions, understand the inputs, make assumptions explicit and communicate what the analysis can—and cannot—tell us.

01

Understand the business

Start with the business model, industry context and drivers that matter.

↗
02

Work from the evidence

Use financial statements and data carefully; make the source of each input clear.

↗
03

Explain the assumptions

Show the logic behind a model rather than presenting a number without context.

03 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Building a foundation
in finance & data.

I'm a B.Com. (Hons.) student at The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda, majoring in Finance and Economics.

My original plan was to study physics and mathematics and aim for ISRO—I was fascinated by systems in motion. When I shifted to commerce, I realized finance operates exactly the same way. Whether it's a DCF valuation, accounting principles, or a SQL data pipeline, it's all about how underlying logic and numbers connect to drive a larger system.

That realization pushed me to build practical projects over the last ten months, rather than just studying theory. Fast forward five years, my goal is to be essential in the decision-making of a firm—translating raw data and complex valuation into clear, actionable strategy.

Currently studying: Practical Statistics for Data Scientists (2nd Ed) — focusing on probability distributions, hypothesis testing, and regression.
